# OpenAI levels and how the interview changes by level

Candidates report OpenAI engineering levels that run from L2 to L6. Senior and staff candidates are reported to go through the same loop as other engineers. The level follows your performance in the loop, not the title you applied for, and team matching calls confirm it near the end.

The published sources report the range of levels only. They do not report an official title or a scope definition for each level, so this page does not assign any.

## What is reported

| Topic | What candidates report |
|---|---|
| Level range | L2 to L6 |
| Loop for senior and staff candidates | The same loop as other engineering candidates |
| How the level is set | By performance in the interviews |
| Where the level is confirmed | In team matching calls, after a pass |
| Research roles | A separate loop with added rounds; see [Roles](roles.md) |

## How the interview changes by level

The rounds stay the same, but the evidence that interviewers look for changes. The differences below are the ones that sources report.

| Round | What changes for senior candidates |
|---|---|
| System design | It counts for more in senior roles, and it can appear twice |
| System design questions | Enterprise search built on a large language model is reported most often for senior candidates in final loops |
| Follow-up questions | Scoping and answers to follow-up questions matter more than the first diagram |
| Behavioral | A considered answer about AI risk and responsibility is reported to distinguish senior candidates |
| Project walkthrough | Interviewers ask for a credible path from your project's size to very large demand |
| Coding | The same practical format; a weak coding score is reported to cost the offer at any level |

## What this means for preparation

- Do not prepare for a title. Prepare to show the scope of your real work, because the level is decided from the evidence in the rounds.
- Choose one project you owned from start to end, and prepare its numbers: request volume, data size, latency, and team size.
- Prepare the part of the project that would fail first under 10 times the load, and what you would change.
- Practice the deep section of a design answer. Interviewers pick one component and ask why, what breaks, and how you know.
- Treat team matching as part of the evaluation. Ask each team what it owns and what it released in the last quarter.

## Questions to ask the recruiter

The level is confirmed late, so ask about it early. Three questions are useful.

1. Which level range is this role open for?
2. Does my loop include an early system design round or a project walkthrough?
3. When in the process is the level decided, and who decides it?

Recruiters are reported to share the content of a specific loop when asked. A down-level is an offer at a lower level than the one you interviewed for. If that happens, ask which round produced the weaker evidence before you respond.
